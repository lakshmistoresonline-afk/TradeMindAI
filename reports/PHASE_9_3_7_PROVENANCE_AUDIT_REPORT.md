## 5. Global One-Active-Signal-Per-Stock Invariant Audit

- **Application Logic:** `SignalLedgerService` and `EquitySignalGenerationService` check for existing active signals before creation.
- **Database Enforcement:** Application-level checks exist, but a robust atomic database-level unique constraint across all horizons and strategies requires a composite partial unique index (`symbol`) where `status = 'ACTIVE'` in PostgreSQL.
- **Gap Identified:** SQLite / SQLAlchemy default local operational database relies on application-level transactional uniqueness rather than a strict database-enforced partial unique constraint.
