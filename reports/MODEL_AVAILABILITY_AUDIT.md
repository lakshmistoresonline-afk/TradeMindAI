# TradeMind AI: Model Availability Audit

## 1. Inference Coverage
Analyzed the availability of ML predictions across the NIFTY-200 universe:

| Category | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Total Predictions (Population)** | 7500 |
| **Universe Breadth** | 200 Symbols |
| **High Availability Symbols** | RELIANCE (2119), ACC (1300), TCS (936) |
| **Low Availability Symbols** | NYKAA (1), OBEROIRLTY (1), ONGC (1) |

## 2. Audit Findings
- **Data Skew**: Performance validation is heavily weighted toward high-availability symbols (RELIANCE, ACC, TCS). Over 50% of the total prediction volume is generated by fewer than 5% of the universe.
- **Missing Periods**: Significant gaps exist for many symbols where models were not available or were rejected due to data quality issues. These periods are silently excluded from validation.
- **Selection Bias Risk**: Strategy V2.2 may appear more successful because it only generates signals during periods of high data confidence/availability.

## 3. Impact Statement
- **Non-Uniformity**: Performance metrics should be interpreted as "Performance of V2.2 on available high-confidence data" rather than "Uniform performance across NIFTY-200."
- **Recommendation**: Expand data ingestion to stabilize model availability for the remaining 180+ symbols in the universe.
