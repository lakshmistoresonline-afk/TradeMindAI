# TradeMind AI

Evidence-driven market signal intelligence for Indian equities.

## Product Focus
TradeMind AI exists for one core purpose: presenting auditable, evidence-backed market signals for SHORT, SWING, and LONG horizons.

## Core Pillars
- **DISCOVER**: Identify institutional-grade momentum setups across the NIFTY-200 universe.
- **UNDERSTAND**: Analyze the machine-learning evidence and structural logic behind every signal.
- **TRACK**: Monitor signal evolution in real-time from publication to terminal state.
- **VERIFY**: Audit a 100% transparent historical ledger of observed outcomes.

## Safety & Safety
- **REAL TRADING = FALSE**: TradeMind AI is an intelligence-only platform. No real broker integration or order execution is enabled.
- **Zero Fabrication**: 100% of signals are traceable to machine predictions and bitwise decision hashes.
