# TradeMind AI: USER TRUST & TRANSPARENCY REPORT

## 🛡️ Building Institutional Confidence
Trust is the foundation of TradeMind AI. We have implemented several "Trust Anchors" to ensure users can rely on our AI-assisted research.

## 1. Thesis Transparency (No Black Box)
- **Why/Why Not Engine**: Displays the exact bullish and bearish factors identified by 12 independent analyst agents.
- **Evidence-First AI**: Every recommendation cites specific technical levels (e.g., "Order Block at ₹2450") or fundamental ratios.
- **Invalidation Conditions**: The AI explicitly states when its thesis is wrong, providing a clear "safety exit" for the user.

## 2. Accuracy Auditing
- **10Y Backtest Hub**: Users can verify the historical win rate and profit factor of the AI strategy for every symbol.
- **Recommendation History**: A transparent ledger of all past AI ratings and their 30-day outcomes.
- **Forensic Logs**: Real-time worker logs are visible in the Admin Control Center for technical accountability.

## 3. Ethical Disclosures
- Standardized risk disclosures implemented across all research reports.
- Clearly distinguished between **Facts** (OHLC, Ratios) and **AI Predictions** (Bias, Target).
- Zero "guaranteed return" claims.
