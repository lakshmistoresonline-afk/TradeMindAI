# TradeMind AI: UI/UX EXCELLENCE REVIEW

## 🎨 Visual Identity & Information Architecture
TradeMind AI v2.0 provides a premium, institutional-grade research environment that reduces cognitive load while maintaining high information density.

## 1. Information Hierarchy Improvements
- **Analysis Page**: Shifted from "Data-First" to "Decision-First." The primary trade recommendation and risk/reward are now the first things a user sees.
- **Dashboard**: Prioritized Market Breadth (A/D Ratio) as the lead indicator for overall market health.
- **Sidebar**: Standardized icons and typography to ensure consistent navigation across 18+ research views.

- Implemented smooth transitions between research tabs.
