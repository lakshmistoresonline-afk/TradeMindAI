# TradeMind AI: BEFORE vs AFTER REDESIGN

## 📊 Dashboard Evolution

### BEFORE (RC-3)
- Dispersed stat cards for Nifty, Bank Nifty, and VIX.
- Market Breadth was a standalone card with significant whitespace.
- Navigation was a flat list of 18 items.
- Signal feed was a simple list with limited interaction.

### AFTER (Product Excellence)
- **Unified Market Pulse**: A single high-density header providing index performance, session breadth, and FII flows in 3 seconds.
- **Grouped Navigation**: Logical categorization into Market Radar, Intelligence Hub, and Strategy.
- **Interactive Signal Feed**: Clicking a signal now slides out a "Quick Research" drawer instead of forcing a page change.

---

## 🔬 Stock Analysis Evolution

### BEFORE (RC-3)
- Infinite scroll required to find risk or financials.
- BUY/SELL rating disappeared the moment the user scrolled down.
- Data was presented in a generic sans-serif font.

### AFTER (Product Excellence)
- **Persistent Anchor**: The Institutional Rating, Target, and Stop-loss stay fixed at the top of the screen.
- **Four Research Perspectives**:
  1. `OVERVIEW`: Executive summary and signals.
  2. `FORENSICS`: Digital Twin and Knowledge Graph.
  3. `HEALTH`: Fundamentals and Moat.
  4. `HISTORY`: Backtest and Signal Audit.
- **Institutional Typography**: All pricing data uses **JetBrains Mono** for maximum precision and trust.
