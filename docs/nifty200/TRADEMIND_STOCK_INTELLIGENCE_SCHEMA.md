# TRADEMIND AI: STOCK INTELLIGENCE SCHEMA

## 1. Structured Stock Profile
- **Trend Score**: 0.0 to 1.0 (EMA 20/50/200 Alignment)
- **Momentum Score**: RSI-derived rate of change.
- **Volatility Score**: ATR-to-Price ratio.
- **Relative Strength**: Stock performance vs NIFTY 200.

## 2. Technical Structure (JSON)
- **Primary Trend**: UP / DOWN / SIDEWAYS
- **Support Levels**: Historically significant buy zones.
- **Resistance Levels**: Historically significant sell zones.
- **Overbought/Oversold**: RSI-based threshold status.

## 3. Composite Intelligence
- **Intelligence Score**: Aggregated signal of all technical, fundamental, and institutional factors.
- **Context**: Linking stock behavior to its sector regime and broader market regime.
