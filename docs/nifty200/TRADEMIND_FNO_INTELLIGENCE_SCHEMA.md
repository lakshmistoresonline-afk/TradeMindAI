# TRADEMIND AI: F&O INTELLIGENCE SCHEMA

## 1. Futures Intelligence
- **Basis**: Difference between Spot and Near Future.
- **Cost of Carry**: Implied rate from basis and time to expiry.
- **OI Change**: Day-over-day change in Open Interest.
- **Rollback Rate**: Near to Next month roll-over percentage.

## 2. Options Intelligence
- **PCR (Put-Call Ratio)**: Volume and OI based sentiment.
- **Max Pain**: Price level where most option buyers lose premium.
- **IV (Implied Volatility)**: Current IV vs 52-week rank.
- **Strike Concentration**: Identification of gamma walls.
