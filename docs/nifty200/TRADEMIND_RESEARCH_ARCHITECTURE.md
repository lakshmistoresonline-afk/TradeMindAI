# TRADEMIND AI: RESEARCH ARCHITECTURE

## 1. Research Workflow
TradeMind AI provides a non-linear, multi-tier research architecture that allows users to move from broad market trends to symbol-specific forensic evidence.

## 2. Market Command Center
The aggregate view of the NIFTY 200 health.
- **Indices**: NIFTY 50 and BANKNIFTY live tracking.
- **Regime**: Dynamic classification (BULL, BEAR, SIDEWAYS).
- **Sentiment**: Bullish/Bearish score (0.0 to 1.0).
- **Institutional**: FII/DII flow magnitude and directional bias.

## 3. Stock Research Terminal
A dedicated forensic environment for individual constituents.
- **Evidence Matrix**: Confluence of Technical, Fundamental, and Institutional factors.
- **Provenance**: Direct links to the ML predictions and snapshots used for decisions.
- **Contextual Signals**: Mapping stock performance against its sector and market regime.

## 4. Opportunity Radar
The discovery layer focused on high-probability setups before a V2.2 signal is triggered.
- **Categories**: Momentum, Breakout, Relative Strength, Volume Spikes.
- **Traceability**: Every discovered opportunity is auditable with its own "Evidence Thesis".

## 5. Temporal Integrity
All research components support **Historical Reconstruction**, ensuring that past-dated analysis only uses data available at the target timestamp, effectively preventing look-ahead bias in human-led research.
